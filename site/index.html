About

This site grew out of the Druid "Wild Shape" Beast List Google Sheet by u/spiralout154 (last updated October 2020, 2014 rules). It adds the 2024 rules, the beasts and changes published since, Circle of the Moon at every level, and a short description and tip for each creature.

Stat blocks marked SRD 5.1 or SRD 5.2 come from the System Reference Documents 5.1 and 5.2 by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at dnd.wizards.com and licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. They were retrieved through the Open5e API. A few 2014 Monster Manual beasts that aren't in the SRD (marked summary) show only the key numbers from the original sheet. Creature descriptions and tips were written for this site. Unofficial fan content; not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast.
