Wild Shape Beast List

Every beast a druid can become, for 2014 and 2024 D&D 5e rules

Changes

What has changed on the Wild Shape Beast List, newest first. Back to the beast list.

November 13, 2026

  • Spell Planner link. The footer now links to the new Spell Planner.
  • Printing and polish. A Print my forms button prints your starred forms with their stats, and printing any view leaves out the controls and filters. Links shared in chats now show a proper title and summary, a missing page shows a helpful "page not found" page, and the header and footer links are easier to tap.
  • Combat tracker. Press Transform on any beast to track it in a panel at the bottom of the screen: beast HP (2014) or Wild Shape temp HP (2024), rounds, and Wild Shape uses left. It has Damage, Heal and Revert buttons, attack and damage rolls for each of the beast's attacks, short and long rests, and a short log. Each profile keeps its own tracker.
  • Update notice. If the site is updated while you have it open, a small bar offers to reload so you get the new version.
  • Consistent design. The change log moved to its own page, and the header, footer and theme are now shared across the D&D Toolbox sites.
  • D&D Toolbox link. A link at the top of the page (and in About) goes back to the D&D Toolbox home page.
  • Damage, features, shareable filters. Every beast shows a rough damage per round (DPR: every attack hits, every save fails), and the list can be sorted by it. New feature chips filter by Darkvision, Blindsight, Pack Tactics, Breathes water, Charge / pounce, Keen senses, Spider Climb and Multiattack, with live counts. The link now keeps your filters (search, movement, features, size, books, view and sort), so a filtered list can be bookmarked or shared.
  • Core books first, live counts. The Sources picker lists the core books first (the SRDs and the Monster Manuals) and starts with only those selected; a Core books button goes back to that. The movement chips and size menu show how many beasts each would give you. Searching lists name matches first. A Reset filters button clears search, movement, size and the book selection. Filter menus stay above the cards on phones, and each update loads fresh instead of an old cached copy.
  • Phone fix. On narrow screens the Sources and profile menus no longer slip behind the beast cards.
  • New address. The site moved to wernercd.github.io/dnd-druid-wildshapes. Your saved profiles and notes carry over.
  • Profiles, hiding and notes. Keep several druids, each with their own starred forms, hidden beasts, notes and settings. Hide any beast from the list (eye icon), and show hidden ones again. Add notes to a beast in its detail view. Export and import a backup file. Added this change log, the source code link and the source material links.
  • Book colours. Each source book has its own colour: a coloured edge on cards, a coloured source tag, and a matching dot in the Sources picker.
  • Card detail and sources. Basic / Stats / Full card views, the source on every card, a searchable Sources picker, and the "One list (all books)" toggle.
  • Launch. 2014 and 2024 rules, standard and Circle of the Moon druids at every level, full SRD stat blocks, a description and tip for each beast, starred known forms, and an index of beasts from other books.
